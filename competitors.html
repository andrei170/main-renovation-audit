The One Competitor You Should Actually Worry About
Barrelle Roofing — 147 Lee Byrd Rd, Loganville

They have an address in your town, a page for your town, and twenty more town pages around it.

And they are the only firm in this comparison running a Google Ads conversion tag and a Meta pixel. That means when they buy a click, Google learns from it and they can follow that visitor around afterwards.

This is exactly what you told Ramses you want to start doing — except they built the pages and the tracking first, and you would be starting with neither.

You would be bidding against a firm in your own town that has a landing page for every search term you want and a bidding algorithm that has been learning for months. Not impossible. But not something to walk into blind.

What They Built That You Could Build In Weeks
Barrelle's 21 town pages: loganville · snellville · monroe · conyers · dacula · lawrenceville lilburn · duluth · suwanee · winder · watkinsville · norcross decatur · tucker · redan · belvedere-park · mcdonough athens · greensboro · covington · atlanta The first twelve are almost exactly the radius you described to us. You have a page for none of them.
Notice what is missing from their list, and what it tells you

Atlanta is one page out of twenty-one for them. They are not fighting for Atlanta — they are winning the towns around it, which is precisely the strategy you already described and have not yet implemented.

Your own site does the opposite: Atlanta is mentioned twelve times in your copy and Loganville once.

Where You Can Win
This field has real gaps and you are positioned for two of them
  • Nobody here publishes commercial depth. These are residential-first roofers. You are Conklin-trained in spray foam, coatings and single-ply — a genuine commercial specialism. Your commercial page is 212 words with no form, which is the cheapest fix in either document.
  • Two of the three have no advertising tags at all. Only Barrelle is properly instrumented. This is not a sophisticated field — it is one prepared firm and several unprepared ones.
  • You have proper RoofingContractor structured data. That is a real technical advantage you already hold.
  • Insurance-claim roofing is already one of your declared services, and in a hail state that is the highest-intent search there is.
  • Twenty-one town pages is a finite job, not a moat. Barrelle did it once and it has been working for them ever since. The same work is available to you.
And where they are genuinely ahead

The Roof Clinic publishes 112 pages including 28 portfolio items and case studies, and states HAAG certification and Xactimate training. On insurance work that is exactly the credibility signal a homeowner looks for, and it is published where yours is not.

Bryant Roofing has been in Loganville since 1989. You will not out-tenure them, so do not try — out-specify them on commercial, where they are thin.

What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • Everyone's Google Business Profile, review count and map position, including yours. On local roofing that is where a large share of enquiry begins and we have no reliable way to measure it.
  • What anyone actually spends on Google Ads, what they bid on, or what it returns. We can see that Barrelle has the tag installed. We cannot see their budget, their keywords or their results.
  • Anyone's revenue, crew size, pricing, close rate or insurance-claim volume, including yours.
  • Whether any firm here holds the certifications it displays — we read websites, not registers.
  • Every roofer within your radius. This is the set we could find and verify, not a census, and it excludes the storm-chasers who arrive after a hail event and leave again.

A firm on Lee Byrd Road has twenty-one town pages, a Google Ads tag and a pixel.

You have the credentials, the commercial specialism and the schema. You just have not written the pages yet.
